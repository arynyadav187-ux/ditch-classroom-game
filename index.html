HOW TO PLAY DITCH CLASSROOM

🕹️ Controls (Desktop & Mobile Portrait): Move with W A S D / Arrow Keys or the on-screen touch pad. Press E (or INTERACT) near computers, bookshelves, lab machines, or locked doors. Use SPACE (or JUMP) in runner/rooftop levels and SHIFT (or BOOST) for a speed burst.
❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️ 5 Hearts & Suspicion Meter: You start every level with 5 Hearts. Entering a Professor's vision cone fills the Suspicion Meter. At 100%, or on touching an obstacle, you lose 1 heart and get 1.5 seconds of blinking invincibility.
🤓 Easy vs. Hard Mode ("Nerd Alert"): In Hard Mode, Class Toppers (🤓) patrol the rooms! If a Nerd spots you, they snitch to the professor and rapidly spike your Suspicion Meter.
🔀 Randomized Lanes & Stream Riddles: Every attempt randomizes the entry and exit lanes so they never line up in an easy straight line. Solve quick math & stream puzzles to unlock secured doors!
